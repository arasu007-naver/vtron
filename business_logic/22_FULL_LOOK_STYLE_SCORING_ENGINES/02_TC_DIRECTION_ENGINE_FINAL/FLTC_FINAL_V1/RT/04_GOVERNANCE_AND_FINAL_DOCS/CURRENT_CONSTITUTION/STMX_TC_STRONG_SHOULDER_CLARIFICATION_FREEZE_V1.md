## 1. CEO decision (verbatim, order V2.3.86 §2)

A Strong / structured shoulder is NOT automatically a Transformative Departure.

Within Jacket / Coat tailoring, a visibly strong, structured, built-up, or emphasized shoulder may remain within conventional tailored grammar.

A shoulder becomes a Transformative Departure only when the actual realization materially departs from conventional garment grammar through governed silhouette, proportion, construction, or another already-authorized Departure mechanism.

Explicitly rejected: **Strong Shoulder = Transformative.**

## 2. Principle (order §5)

The morphology label is evidence, not a Direction verdict. Departure requires a governed departure from conventional grammar. Extended, exaggerated, reconstructed, disproportionate or otherwise materially non-conventional shoulder realizations remain recognisable through their lawful Silhouette / Proportion / Construction evidence (e.g. Oversized fit, Proportion envelope, constitutive construction). No shoulder scoring layer exists.
