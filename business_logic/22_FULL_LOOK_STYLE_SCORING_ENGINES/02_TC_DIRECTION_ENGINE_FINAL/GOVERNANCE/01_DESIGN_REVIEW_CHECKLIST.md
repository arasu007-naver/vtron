## Summary of the review

The 9 V2 `DIRECTION_UNRESOLVED` outcomes all come from one path: the V2 resolver withholds a direction whenever any garment is not fully proven (TC-P2) or the frame has a limit (TC-PV). AVAILABLE_EVIDENCE_MANDATORY_SCORING (CEO 2026-10-24, STMX-2026-10-AEMS) forbids that for scorable inputs. The correction is decided **inside the approved authorities**: T only through a frozen-valid product departure (Product-Only · frozen Step 2); C only when observed product facts are read as conventional by the frozen predicates (missing facts contribute nothing and are never Classic evidence); input failure only when the packet carries no evaluable TC product evidence. Packet structure gives **8 scorable + 1 input failure**, but the input failure is **F033** (no identified garment), not REF_000622 (its skirt is fully observed).
