## 3. CORRECTION B — COMMERCIAL SUIT MEMBER ASSOCIATION

### Confirmed problem

REF_000841 and REF_000810 failed before scoring.

The Vision Extractor identified a partially obscured Trouser/Skirt as not independently reconstructable.

The integration path then failed to construct the pre-declared commercial Suit scoring unit.

### Existing CEO rule

A commercially designated Suit is ONE merchandise scoring unit.

Product membership comes from authoritative product information or explicit user designation.

Visual extraction determines what can actually be observed.

**A partially hidden Suit member does not automatically terminate the merchandise identity of the Suit.**

### Required correction

At the integration boundary:

1. Preserve the authoritative pre-declared Suit identity.
2. Preserve the Jacket and Bottom member identities.
3. Preserve the actual Vision observations and their visibility states.
4. Do not infer any unobserved Bottom measurements, shape, colour or designed details.
5. Pass only contract-valid, actually available evidence through the existing Suit scoring path.
6. Do not remove the Suit merely because its Bottom is not eligible as an independent product scoring unit.

Distinguish:

- **Commercial Suit membership**
- **Independent garment scoring eligibility**
- **Available member-level visual evidence**

These are not interchangeable.

### Implementation limits

Do not change Item Vision V1.3.

Do not change the frozen garment eligibility vocabulary or visual observation values.

Do not change the Suit Scoring Constitutions.

Do not fabricate a missing garment observation block to satisfy a schema.

If a narrow integration successor is needed because R4 is sealed, create only that successor and preserve R4 unchanged.

If an existing Frozen scorer cannot legally accept the available evidence, identify that precise incompatibility rather than silently bypassing its contract.

### Expected outcome

REF_000841 and REF_000810 should reach the existing Suit Scoring Engine whenever their actual evidence satisfies the governed input contract.

The intention is not to reconstruct invisible clothing.

The intention is to prevent the integration layer from rejecting an otherwise established commercial Suit solely because one member cannot be scored independently.
