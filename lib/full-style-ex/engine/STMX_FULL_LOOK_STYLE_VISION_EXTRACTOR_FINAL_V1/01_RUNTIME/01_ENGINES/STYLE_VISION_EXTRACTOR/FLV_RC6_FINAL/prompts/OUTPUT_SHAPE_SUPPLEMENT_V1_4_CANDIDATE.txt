8b. STATE SEMANTICS (transport only; the meanings in section 1 are unchanged)
  · A fact whose value is a governed token "None" (asymmetry None · pocket None · shoulder_drop None · collar None · slit None · shoulder_structure None) is OBSERVED with that value — it is not ABSENT.
  · ABSENT is only for a visible carrier that does not show the phenomenon on a fact whose domain has no "None" token (pattern · graphic · surface · material · decorative_detail · attachment · exposure rows).
  · A fact you do not emit is read as UNKNOWN, never as ABSENT. Emit every applicable fact of every garment (section 6) and the six appearance facts of every accessory.
  · material · surface · decorative_detail · attachment are MULTI facts: always an array when OBSERVED. Every other fact is one value.
  (No colour-reading rule is added here: CDM-08 "black reads dark" was withdrawn; lightness is reported as seen under Colour V1.)

8c. OUTPUT FORMAT (transport only)
  · Return the JSON object as raw text: the first character of your reply is "{" and the last character is "}". No markdown code fence (no ```), no "json" label, no prose before or after, no wrapper object, no comments, no trailing commentary. Exactly one object.

8d. COMPONENT IDENTITY VERSUS ATTRIBUTE VISIBILITY (clarifies sections 1, 3 and 7; category meanings are those of Item Vision V1_3, unchanged)
  · Identity is decided from the part of the garment that IS visible. When the visible part supports one Item category (e.g. a ribbed knit turtleneck collar → Sweater; a woven collar with a button placket → Shirt), report that category as OBSERVED even if most of the garment is hidden by another garment.
  · A hidden property never lowers the identity. Report each hidden fact with its own state: fit · length · sleeve · shoulder facts · pocket · closure · front opening as NOT_VISIBLE with the limitation (OCCLUDED_BY_OTHER_GARMENT · FRAGMENTARY_VISIBILITY · TERMINAL_REGION_CROPPED); material · surface as UNKNOWN when they cannot be told apart. Identity and attribute visibility are evaluated independently.
  · Identity UNKNOWN is reserved for a garment whose visible part fits no single Item category (section 3: cape, poncho, bra top …) or for two categories that remain equally supported after the visible evidence (knit vs woven · collar · closure · hem) has been used. "Only a small part is visible" is not by itself a reason for UNKNOWN.
  · Never assign a category the visible evidence does not support, and never fill a hidden fact with a guess.

8e. INTEGRATED SELF-BELT (one rule; reads together with section 5 SVP-011, the section 5 "Rules" sentence and the section 6 belt firewall — CDM-03 S1 · CDM-04 B)
  · A belt, tie or sash that belongs to a garment — threaded through its own loops, sewn to it, or the garment's own closure device knotted at the waist — is PART OF THAT GARMENT. It is NOT a component: do not list it in objects, do not make it a colour carrier, do not treat it as an accessory. A difference in colour, material or texture between the belt and the garment body does not by itself make it a separate object.
  · A belt is a separate belt component ONLY when it is a distinct object: its own buckle / closure hardware or an independent band worn over the garment, not constructionally part of it.
  · Record an integrated belt on its garment: facts.<garment>.attachment includes "Belt"; its colour is a point colour area of the garment with part {"carrier_kind": "attachment", "carrier_ref": "Belt"}; the garment's waist_definition is Cinched when the belt actually draws the waist in, Defined when the waist is shaped without draw-in, Undefined otherwise.
  · Report the belt's securing action ONCE, in securing_relations, in the own-device form { "affected": "<garment>", "device_is_own": true, "region": "<where the draw-in occurs, e.g. waist>", "visible_constriction": "OBSERVED|ABSENT|UNKNOWN" } — OBSERVED only when the contour is actually drawn in; ABSENT when the belt lies on the garment without draw-in; UNKNOWN when you cannot tell. Never add a second securing entry for the same physical cause, and never derive a securing relation that is not visible.
  · Where section 5 SVP-011 says "own belts → SVP-119", read it as: the own belt is reported through the garment's waist_definition and this own-device entry — never through a belt component.
